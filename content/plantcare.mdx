Take or upload a photo of a plant and the app tells you what it is and how to look after it.

Each photo is uploaded, sent to an AI model and the answer is saved as a structured care plan – in one database transaction that also charges a credit. New accounts start with 5 free credits; more can be bought through Stripe.

## Features
- Plant identification from a photo, taken with the camera or uploaded
- Name, scientific name and a short description of the plant
- Care plan split into watering, light, soil, temperature, humidity, fertilization and repotting
- A list of every identified plant with its care plan
- Credit packs through Stripe Checkout and a webhook
