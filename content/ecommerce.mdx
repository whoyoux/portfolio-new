An online shop built end to end: catalog, cart, checkout, orders and an admin panel, with "Fresh & Clean" as the demo store.

The interesting part is the order flow – stock is checked before checkout, payment goes through Stripe Checkout with shipping options, and a webhook turns a paid session into an order.

## Features
- Catalog with categories, search and product pages
- Cart and wishlist
- Email/password accounts with activation links and a shipping-address profile
- Stripe Checkout with shipping options and a webhook that creates the order
- Order history with a page per order
- Admin panel for products, orders and customers, gated by a user role
- Stock and sold counters per product
