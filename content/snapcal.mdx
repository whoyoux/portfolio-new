Take a photo of your meal and get its calories. A small app I built to try structured output from a vision model.

The photo is compressed with sharp, uploaded through UploadThing and sent to GPT-4o mini through the Vercel AI SDK with a Zod schema – so the answer comes back as typed JSON: meal name, calories, carbs, protein, fat and ingredients. It is saved to Postgres and shows up in your meal history.

## Features
- Calories and macros (carbs, protein, fat) from a single photo
- Meal history with a page per meal
- Delete a meal
- Installable as a PWA
- Auth with better-auth, Prisma on Postgres
